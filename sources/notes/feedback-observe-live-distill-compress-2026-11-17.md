Urs (2026-11-17, during the gap-closing rounds): "Less refusals, less trials, less gates, less doors, more real time observation with zero impact, more end to end real flows, less fixed rows and static tissues, more distillation, more abstractions, more generic learning, more compression."

**Why:** the rounds had grown a lattice of per-request witnesses, closure rows, band bits and gates (each fix adding a new check), and four flow requests failed in one identical way (band-compile-error) because the cause was generic while the tissue was per-row. He wants the body to learn the general shape, not carry one row per instance.

**How to apply:** when a failure repeats, dissolve the generic cause once (and distil it into a teaching/abstraction) instead of adding a row, gate or door per instance; watch flows through the glass / shared-memory signals (no extra process, no extra GPU cost) rather than by running more trial passes; prefer one real end-to-end flow (plan→candidate→band→land on the resident 27B) over several staged checks; fold duplicate rows and doors into one generic mechanism; compress ledgers into distillation rows.
